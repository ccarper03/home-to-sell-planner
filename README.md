# Home-to-Sell Planner

A sales-table tool for buyers who have to sell their current home before they buy new construction.

Enter the buyer's situation and it:

- Ranks every route that fits: buy first and sell after, a home-to-sell contingency, a home-to-close contingency, selling first with a rent-back, selling first and staying in between, Knock, Opendoor, Mark Spain, or keeping the home as a rental
- Shows how many times they'd move, how long they'd carry two payments or sit between homes, how certain each route is, and a rough net from the sale
- Draws the move month by month: the old home, the new home, where they live and their payments
- Drafts a dated plan to text the buyer and a list of follow-ups to log in CRM

Nothing a buyer enters is saved. Fees and timing under "Assumptions and sources" are examples to replace with real quotes.

Planning estimates only, not financial or legal advice. Confirm program terms with each provider, and the numbers with the lender and listing agent.
