    Assumptions and sources
    Sources, opened October 5, 2026 (Knock updated October 18, 2026)
    • Selling to Opendoor vs. a traditional sale, Opendoor: close in 21 to 60 days, closing costs 1 to 3%
    • Opendoor fees, Real Estate Witch: 5% example fee, repairs extra
    • Knock Bridge Loan guide and July 2026 matrix, Knock: 2.25% contract fee plus about $1,850 in closing costs, 0% interest for 6 months, total debt on the old home up to 75% of its value, old home worth $150,000 or more, up to $35,000 for prep and $5,000 for moving, list within 45 days of the new closing, backup offer if unsold after 6 months
    • Guaranteed Offer program, Mark Spain: serves Raleigh, offers in about 5 business days, close in as few as 21 days
    • Mark Spain review, Real Estate Witch: listing agreement required, about 3.5% commission, cash offers 60 to 80% of value
    Planning estimates, not financial or legal advice. Program terms change, so confirm fees and eligibility with each provider, and the numbers with the lender and listing agent. D.R. Horton contingency terms follow the purchase agreement.
